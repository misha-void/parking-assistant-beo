# Parking Pricing and Operating Hours in Belgrade

This document explains the pricing structure and operating hours for parking control and payment across different zones in Belgrade.

## Operating Hours by Zone

Parking control and payment requirements vary by zone and day of the week.

### Purple Zone (A), Red Zone (1), and White Zone (B)

**Monday to Saturday:**
- Control hours: 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM
- Payment required during these hours

**Sunday:**
- Control hours: 7:00 AM to 2:00 PM
- Payment required during these hours

**Outside control hours:** Parking is free, but spots may still be limited.

### Yellow Zone (2) and Green Zone (3)

**Monday to Friday:**
- Control hours: 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM
- Payment required during these hours

**Saturday:**
- Control hours: 7:00 AM to 2:00 PM
- Payment required during these hours

**Sunday:** 
- No parking control
- Parking is free all day

## Pricing Structure

Parking rates are charged **per hour** and vary by zone. Prices are in **Serbian Dinars (RSD)**.

### Typical Rates (Note: Rates may change - check official sources for current prices)

- **Zone A (Purple)**: Highest rate - short-term parking only
- **Zone 1 (Red)**: High rate - city center
- **Zone B (White)**: Medium-high rate
- **Zone 2 (Yellow)**: Medium rate
- **Zone 3 (Green)**: Lowest rate - residential areas

*Exact pricing is managed by JKP "Parking servis" and may be adjusted periodically.*

## Payment Methods

Multiple convenient payment options are available:

1. **Mobile Apps**: Official parking app (recommended)
2. **SMS**: Send a text message with your zone and plate number
3. **Parking Meters**: Available at some locations
4. **Phone Call**: Automated payment line

## Extension Rules

- **Zone A (Purple)**: No extensions available
- **Zone 1 (Red)**: 30-minute extension possible
- **Zones B, 2, 3 (White, Yellow, Green)**: 60-minute extension possible

**Important:** Extensions must be purchased **before** your original parking time expires. You cannot extend after expiration.

## Special Free Parking

### Clinical Centre Area (Zone 3)
Approximately 400 parking spots near the Clinical Centre of Serbia are **free after 5:00 PM** on weekdays (Monday-Friday).

**Free parking streets:**
- Pasterova (from Resavska to Bulevar oslobođenja)
- Dr Subotića (from Deligradska to Bulevar oslobođenja)
- Deligradska (from Pasterova to Dr Subotića)

## Penalties

Parking violations result in fines. Common violations include:
- Parking without payment
- Exceeding maximum parking duration
- Parking in restricted areas or zones

**Note:** Always ensure your payment is active and your vehicle is in an authorized spot.

## Public Holidays

On public holidays, special schedules may apply. Check the official JKP "Parking servis" website or app for holiday parking information.
