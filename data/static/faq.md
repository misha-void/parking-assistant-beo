# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### What is the parking zone system in Belgrade?
Belgrade uses a color-coded zone system with five zones: Purple (Zone A), Red (Zone 1), White (Zone B), Yellow (Zone 2), and Green (Zone 3). Each zone has different time limits and pricing. Zone A allows only 30 minutes, while Zone 3 allows up to 3 hours.

### How do I know which zone I'm in?
Zones are marked with colored signs and pavement markings. Look for posts or signs displaying the zone color and letter/number (e.g., "Zone A - Purple" or "Zone 3 - Green").

### Can I park for free anywhere in Belgrade?
Yes, parking is free outside of controlled hours. Purple, Red, and White zones are free after 10 PM on weekdays and after 2 PM on Sundays. Yellow and Green zones are free after 9 PM on weekdays, after 2 PM on Saturdays, and all day Sunday. Additionally, some areas near the Clinical Centre are free after 5 PM on weekdays.

## Payment Questions

### How do I pay for parking?
You can pay using:
- Mobile apps (official parking app)
- SMS (send your zone and plate number)
- Parking meters (at some locations)
- Automated phone line

### What payment methods are accepted?
Mobile app and phone payments typically accept credit/debit cards. Parking meters may accept coins, cards, or contactless payment depending on the model.

### Do I need to display a ticket?
If you pay by app or SMS, you don't need to display anything - the system records your plate number. If you use a parking meter, place the ticket visibly on your dashboard.

### Can I pay with cash?
Some older parking meters accept coins. For zones without meters, you'll need to use the app, SMS, or phone payment (which require electronic payment methods).

## Time Limits and Extensions

### What happens if I exceed the maximum parking time?
You must move your vehicle to a different zone or wait 30 minutes before parking in the same zone again. Staying beyond the limit may result in a fine.

### Can I extend my parking time?
Yes, most zones allow extensions:
- Zone 1 (Red): 30-minute extension
- Zones B, 2, 3 (White, Yellow, Green): 60-minute extension
- Zone A (Purple): No extensions

Extensions must be purchased **before** your original time expires.

### How do I extend my parking?
Use the same method you used for initial payment (app, SMS, or phone). The extension will be added to your current session.

## Zones and Restrictions

### What is the 30-minute cooldown period?
After your parking time expires in a zone, you cannot park in that same zone for the next 30 minutes. You must either move to a different zone or wait.

### Can I park in multiple zones during one trip?
Yes, you can park in different zones during the same day. Just pay separately for each zone. Remember the 30-minute cooldown if returning to the same zone.

### Are there any special free parking areas?
Yes, around the Clinical Centre of Serbia, approximately 400 spots in Zone 3 (Green) are free after 5 PM on weekdays (Monday-Friday) in Pasterova, Dr Subotića, and Deligradska streets.

## Garages and Car Parks

### What's the difference between a garage and a car park?
- **Garage**: Indoor, multi-level, covered parking structure with better security and weather protection
- **Car Park**: Outdoor parking lot, usually ground-level, exposed to weather
- **Reserved Garage**: Indoor garage with pre-bookable spots for guaranteed availability

### Do garages have different pricing than street parking?
Yes, garages typically charge by the hour or offer daily/monthly passes. Prices vary by location and are usually higher than street parking due to added security and convenience.

### Can I reserve a parking spot in advance?
Yes, **reserved garages** allow advance booking through online systems or mobile apps. Standard garages and car parks operate on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Tourists and Visitors

### I'm a tourist - can I use Belgrade parking?
Yes! The parking system is open to all vehicles with valid license plates. You can use the mobile app, SMS, or parking meters regardless of whether you're a local or visitor.

### Do I need a Serbian phone number for SMS parking?
Most SMS parking systems work with international numbers, but check the official app or website for details. The mobile app may be more convenient for tourists.

### Is there parking near major tourist attractions?
Yes, most tourist areas (city center, Kalemegdan, Skadarlija, etc.) are in Purple, Red, or White zones with short-term parking available. Garages nearby offer longer-term options.

## Violations and Penalties

### What happens if I don't pay for parking?
Parking control officers patrol regularly. If you haven't paid, you'll receive a fine. The amount depends on the violation and zone.

### Can I dispute a parking fine?
Yes, you can appeal fines by contacting JKP "Parking servis" with evidence (payment receipts, photos, etc.). Follow the instructions on the fine notice.

### What if my parking meter receipt isn't visible?
You must display the receipt clearly on your dashboard. If it's not visible, you may receive a fine even if you paid.

## Technical Issues

### What if the parking app isn't working?
Try using SMS or phone payment as backup methods. If all systems are down, contact JKP "Parking servis" customer support.

### I entered the wrong license plate number - what do I do?
Immediately cancel the payment (if the app allows) and re-enter correctly. If you can't cancel, contact customer support with your payment proof.

### The parking meter is broken - where do I pay?
Use the mobile app or SMS as alternatives. If neither works, note the meter number and report it, then try to find another nearby meter or parking location.
