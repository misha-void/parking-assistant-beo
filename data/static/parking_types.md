# Types of Parking Facilities in Belgrade

Belgrade offers three main types of parking facilities to accommodate different needs and preferences. Each type has distinct characteristics and use cases.

## 1. Garage (Indoor Parking)

Garages are **covered, multi-level parking structures** that provide protected parking for vehicles.

### Characteristics:
- **Indoor/covered**: Protection from weather (rain, snow, sun)
- **Multi-level**: Efficient use of space with multiple floors
- **Security**: Often monitored with cameras and staff
- **Access control**: Entry/exit barriers with ticket systems
- **Payment**: Usually pay-on-exit or pre-paid monthly passes

### Typical Locations:
- City center commercial areas
- Shopping malls
- Business districts
- Mixed-use developments

### Advantages:
- Vehicle protection from weather and vandalism
- Safer for long-term parking
- Climate-controlled in some premium garages
- Often open 24/7

### Use Cases:
- Daily commuters working in the city center
- Long-term parking (all-day or overnight)
- Protection for valuable vehicles
- Visitors to nearby businesses or shopping areas

---

## 2. Car Park (Outdoor Parking Lot)

Car parks are **open-air parking lots** with designated spaces, typically fenced or clearly marked.

### Characteristics:
- **Outdoor/uncovered**: Exposed to weather
- **Ground level**: Usually single-level, some may have 2-3 levels
- **Surface parking**: Paved or unpaved surfaces
- **Fencing/gates**: May have controlled entry or open access
- **Payment**: Pay-and-display, pay-on-exit, or zone-based SMS/app payment

### Typical Locations:
- Near shopping centers and supermarkets
- Residential neighborhoods
- Sports facilities and parks
- Transit hubs (near bus/train stations)

### Advantages:
- Lower cost than garages
- Easier to navigate (no ramps or tight turns)
- Quick entry and exit
- Good for short to medium-term parking

### Use Cases:
- Shopping trips
- Short-term errands
- Event parking (concerts, sports games)
- Park-and-ride for public transport users

---

## 3. Reserved Garage (Pre-Bookable Indoor Parking)

Reserved garages are **indoor parking facilities with pre-bookable spots**, offering guaranteed parking for specific users.

### Characteristics:
- **Indoor/covered**: Same protection as standard garages
- **Reservation system**: Spots can be booked in advance
- **Dedicated spaces**: Assigned parking spots for reserved users
- **Monthly/annual contracts**: Often sold as subscriptions
- **Premium service**: Higher price but guaranteed availability

### Typical Locations:
- Premium office buildings
- Residential complexes (for residents)
- Airports and major transport hubs
- High-demand city center areas

### Advantages:
- **Guaranteed availability**: No searching for spots
- **Time-saving**: Know your spot is waiting
- **Convenience**: Ideal for daily commuters
- **Security**: Often higher security than public garages

### Use Cases:
- Office workers with daily parking needs
- Residents in apartment buildings
- Frequent travelers needing airport parking
- Businesses providing parking for employees or clients

### How Reservations Work:
1. Book online or via mobile app
2. Choose date/time slot (hourly, daily, or monthly)
3. Pay in advance
4. Receive confirmation and access code/QR code
5. Use code to enter reserved spot

## Comparing Parking Types

| Feature | Garage | Car Park | Reserved Garage |
|---------|--------|----------|-----------------|
| **Coverage** | Indoor | Outdoor | Indoor |
| **Booking** | Walk-in or pre-pay | Walk-in | Advance reservation |
| **Price** | Medium-High | Low-Medium | High |
| **Availability** | First-come, first-served | First-come, first-served | Guaranteed |
| **Security** | High | Medium | Very High |
| **Weather Protection** | Yes | No | Yes |

---

## Choosing the Right Parking Type

**Choose a Garage if you:**
- Need weather protection
- Want better security
- Plan to park for several hours or overnight
- Are in a busy city center area

**Choose a Car Park if you:**
- Only need short-term parking
- Want lower cost
- Prefer quick in-and-out access
- Are visiting suburban or less central areas

**Choose a Reserved Garage if you:**
- Park daily at the same location
- Want guaranteed availability
- Need premium security and convenience
- Are willing to pay extra for peace of mind
