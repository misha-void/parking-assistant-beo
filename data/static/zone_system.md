# Belgrade Parking Zone System

Belgrade's on-street parking is organized into **five zones** with different time restrictions. Each zone has specific maximum parking durations and extension rules.

## Zone Overview

### Zone A (Purple Zone)
- **Maximum parking time**: 30 minutes
- **Color coding**: Purple
- **Extension**: Not available
- **Typical locations**: High-traffic commercial areas, government buildings

After your parking time expires in Zone A, you **cannot park in the same zone for the next 30 minutes**.

### Zone 1 (Red Zone)
- **Maximum parking time**: 60 minutes (1 hour)
- **Color coding**: Red
- **Extension**: 30 additional minutes available
- **Typical locations**: City center, business districts

After your parking time expires in Zone 1, you **cannot park in the same zone for the next 30 minutes**.

### Zone B (White Zone)
- **Maximum parking time**: 120 minutes (2 hours)
- **Color coding**: White
- **Extension**: 60 additional minutes available
- **Typical locations**: Mixed residential/commercial areas

After your parking time expires in Zone B, you **cannot park in the same zone for the next 30 minutes**.

### Zone 2 (Yellow Zone)
- **Maximum parking time**: 120 minutes (2 hours)
- **Color coding**: Yellow
- **Extension**: 60 additional minutes available
- **Typical locations**: Residential areas with some commercial activity

After your parking time expires in Zone 2, you **cannot park in the same zone for the next 30 minutes**.

### Zone 3 (Green Zone)
- **Maximum parking time**: 180 minutes (3 hours)
- **Color coding**: Green
- **Extension**: 60 additional minutes available
- **Typical locations**: Residential areas, hospital zones, university districts

After your parking time expires in Zone 3, you **cannot park in the same zone for the next 30 minutes**.

## Special Rules for Clinical Centre Area

Around the Clinical Centre of Serbia, approximately **400 parking spots in Zone 3 (Green Zone)** are **free of charge after 5 PM** on weekdays (Monday to Friday).

These free spots are located in the following streets:
- **Pasterova Street**: from Resavska to Bulevar oslobođenja
- **Dr Subotića Street**: from Deligradska to Bulevar oslobođenja  
- **Deligradska Street**: from Pasterova to Dr Subotića

## Important Notes

- You must move your vehicle to a different zone or wait 30 minutes before parking again in the same zone
- Extensions must be purchased before the original parking time expires
- Parking zones are clearly marked with colored signs and pavement markings
- Mobile apps and SMS payment are available for all zones
